About

I've spent the past year as the automation and back end engineer for a B2B sales intelligence company, building the research pipeline, the CRM tooling and the multi-tenant product you see above. Alongside that I take on builds for founders and small teams: outreach, lead research, onboarding, reporting.

I work in n8n and Python, use Claude where a step needs judgment, and keep everything in your own accounts so nothing depends on me being around.

How I work

We start with what's taking too long. I map the steps, pick the smallest system that removes them and build it, with Claude where a step needs judgment.

Everything logs its own runs, never sends the same thing twice and fails loudly instead of quietly. You get the workflows, the credentials in your accounts and a short doc on how it runs and what to do when it stops. Most builds take a week or two. If it's going to take longer I'll say so before we start.
