How I work

We start with what's taking too long. I map the steps, pick the smallest system that removes them and build it in n8n or Python, with Claude where a step needs judgment.

Everything I build logs its own runs, never sends the same thing twice and fails loudly instead of quietly. You get the workflows, the credentials in your own accounts and a short doc on how it runs and what to do when it stops.

Most builds take a week or two. If it's going to take longer I'll say so before we start.
